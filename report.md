# PizzaTech : Chatbot for Pizzerias

## 1. Introduction

  As part of this project, we developed PizzaTech, an intelligent chatbot designed for pizzerias, with the goal of improving customer service while saving money and optimizing time

---

## 2. Business Analysis

###  Target
- Independent pizzerias
- Restaurants offering delivery or click & collect services

### Identified Problems
- Too many customer calls for simple requests
- Frequent interruptions of kitchen staff
- Loss of time and productivity
- Risk of human errors

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

PizzaTech is a chatbot that allows users to:

- Track their order in real time
-  Get delivery time estimates
-  Automatically answer frequently asked questions

The chatbot is accessible via a messaging platform (Discord) and replaces repetitive phone calls.

---

## 4. Objectives

- Automate customer service
- Reduce phone calls
- Improve user experience
- Optimize staff efficiency

---

## 5. Value Proposition

### Time Saving
Staff are no longer interrupted by repetitive calls.

### Cost Reduction
Less need for phone-based customer support.

### Improved Customer Experience
Instant responses available 24/7.

---

## 6. Value Chain Impact

### Pizzeria Value Chain  
  
| Stage                     | Description                                                      | Chatbot Impact |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 1. Customer Acquisition   | Attracting customers through ads, social media, or word of mouth | Not automated  |
| 2. Order Placement        | Customer places an order via phone, website, or app              | Not automated  |
| 3. Order Confirmation     | Restaurant confirms the order and provides estimated time        | Automated      |
| 4. Order Preparation      | Kitchen staff prepares the pizza                                 | Not automated  |
| 5. Cooking                | Pizza is cooked in the oven                                      | Not automated  |
| 6. Delivery Preparation   | Order is packaged and assigned to a delivery driver              | Not automated  |
| 7. Delivery               | Pizza is delivered to the customer                               | Not automated  |
| 8. Customer Communication | Customer asks for updates (status, delivery time, changes)       | Automated      |
| 9. Order Tracking         | Customer wants to know the status of their order                 | Automated      |
| 10. Order Modification    | Customer requests changes before preparation                     | Not automated  |
| 11. Customer Support      | Handling complaints or questions                                 | Not automated  |
